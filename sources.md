## Considered and dropped

- **Veissière, varieties of tulpa experiences**: could not be resolved to a stable locator; Palmer-Cooper et al. and Taylor et al. carry the cultivated-agent point with peer-reviewed records.
- **Specific closed-loop Bayesian-optimization studies named in the seed**: lookups returned adjacent stimulation work rather than the described studies, so the closed-loop proposal is stated as architecture and cites no optimization result.
- **Muller et al. (2023), data-driven dissection of the fever effect**: a further useful entry in the fever literature; Curran and Byrne bracket the question adequately.
- **FurScience web reports on phantom-limb frequency**: community survey summaries without a peer-reviewed locator for the specific comparison; the phantom-experience point is carried by Grivell et al. and Luiggi-Hernández et al.
- **Petkova and Ehrsson body-swap; Lenggenhager out-of-body**: Blanke, Slater and Serino survey this ground.
- **Friston-style active-inference formulations**: Seth carries the inferential-self point without committing the paper to a specific free-energy formalism.
- **Ehrsson's broader supernumerary corpus, Kilteni et al. reviews**: the third-arm and peripersonal results suffice.

The migration corrected several bibliographic details. The first author of the fever preprint is Katherine Byrne (not "R.
